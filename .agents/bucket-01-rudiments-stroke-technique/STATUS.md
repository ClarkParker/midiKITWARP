  - findings that change the model rather than just feed it:
    (1) the Codex's sticking legend is DYNAMIC, not handed - loud and soft per
        hand - and its items 2 and 3 are the bare pairs loud-soft and soft-loud,
        ranked above the flam. v0.1 cannot say "this attack is the soft half of a
        pair". Hart 1860 defines the flam the same way
    (2) Greissinger 1900 already encodes events as an event letter plus an attack
        count (t/f/d/r with the number under the roll) - the ornament-plus-count
        shape the pivot needs, working in 1900
    (3) stroke types, grips, technique schools and the rudiment names themselves
        map to NO axis: they name motion and sequence, not sound
    (4) PAS renamed its own list between 1958 and 1984, so aliases must span
        revisions of one list, not only rival lists
    (5) Triple Paradiddle is item 18 of an 1864 US Army gamut although PAS counts
        it among its 1984 additions: date a rudiment by its earliest manual
    (6) open means SOFT in Hart 1860; the default lead hand is LEFT in five sources
    (7) Tourte via de Douvan and Hessler's Codex agree on every French-to-American
        pair they share, so the French section is solid on two independent sources
  - gaps against v0.1 named with evidence: gok-shot is misspelt (sources say gock
