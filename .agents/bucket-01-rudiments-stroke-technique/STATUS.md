  - findings that change the model rather than just feed it:
    (1) Greissinger 1900 already encodes drum events as an event letter plus an
        attack count - t/f/d/r with the stroke number written under the roll -
        which is the ornament-plus-count shape the pivot needs, working in 1900
    (2) stroke types, grips, technique schools and the rudiment names themselves
        map to NO axis: they name motion and sequence, not sound. Recorded
        explicitly so nobody adds full-stroke to the technique axis later
    (3) PAS renamed its own list between 1958 and 1984 (Long Roll to Double Stroke
        Open Roll, Ruff to Drag, the two drags gained a Tap), so aliases must span
        revisions of one list, not only rival lists
    (4) Triple Paradiddle is item 18 of an 1864 US Army gamut although PAS counts
        it among its 1984 additions: date a rudiment by its earliest manual
    (5) open means SOFT in Hart 1860, a false friend for the openness axis
    (6) the default lead hand is LEFT in Hart, Strube, Greissinger, Straight and
        modern Italian practice
  - gaps against v0.1 named with evidence: gok-shot is misspelt (sources say gock
