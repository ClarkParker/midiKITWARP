# Bucket 06 — Drum and cymbal manufacturer terminology

Read `.agents/round2/BRIEF.md` first. It defines the three-round method, the axis
model and the output structure. Follow it exactly.

## What this bucket must cover

- Zildjian, Sabian, Paiste and Meinl: their published cymbal anatomy and playing-zone terminology - bow, edge, bell, cup, crash zone, ride zone, profile, taper - and their sound and technique glossaries.
- Remo, Evans and Aquarian drumhead terminology: batter, resonant, collar, bearing edge, muffling, and the named damping accessories.
- Vic Firth, Promark and Vater stick terminology: tip, bead, shoulder, shaft, taper, butt, and the named tip shapes and materials.
- Ludwig, DW, Pearl, Yamaha, Gretsch and Sonor catalogue and manual terminology for kit parts and for named drums (octoban, gong drum, piccolo snare, timbale).
- Latin Percussion, Meinl Percussion and Toca instructional material - their published stroke names for congas, bongos, timbales, cajon and hand percussion.
- Modern Drummer, Drum! and DRUMMER magazine glossaries.
- Percussion retailer taxonomies used to categorise products, which are a de-facto instrument classification.

## Note on this bucket

Vendor glossaries are where the trade words come from, and they are the words the libraries then use in their articulation lists. This bucket explains why libraries spell things the way they do.
