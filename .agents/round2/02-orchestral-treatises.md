# Bucket 02 — Orchestration treatises and percussion writing manuals

Read `.agents/round2/BRIEF.md` first. It defines the three-round method, the axis
model and the output structure. Follow it exactly.

## What this bucket must cover

- James Blades, Percussion Instruments and Their History - the standard reference.
- Samuel Adler, The Study of Orchestration - its percussion chapter and its technique tables.
- Gardner Read, Thesaurus of Orchestral Devices and Compendium of Modern Instrumental Techniques - Read enumerates playing techniques exhaustively and is the single richest source of technique names in print.
- Kurt Stone, Music Notation in the Twentieth Century - the standardised percussion notation and technique terms.
- Peinkofer and Tannigel, Handbook of Percussion Instruments.
- Samuel Solomon, How to Write for Percussion - modern, and explicitly a technique catalogue; his instrument-by-instrument technique lists are exactly the shape needed.
- Reginald Smith Brindle, Contemporary Percussion.
- Berlioz/Strauss Treatise on Instrumentation, and Rimsky-Korsakov - the historical layer.
- Any university percussion-writing guide that publishes a technique table.

## Note on this bucket

Look for published technique tables and glossaries. archive.org, HathiTrust, Google Books previews, university course pages and composer resource sites all carry these.
