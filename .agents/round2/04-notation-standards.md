# Bucket 04 — Notation standards and their technique enumerations

Read `.agents/round2/BRIEF.md` first. It defines the three-round method, the axis
model and the output structure. Follow it exactly.

## What this bucket must cover

- SMuFL 1.4 - the complete percussion pictogram, beater and technique glyph lists with codepoints and canonical names. Clone w3c/smufl and read data/ rather than the website.
- MusicXML 4.0 - percussion element, stick-type, stick-material, beater-value, stick-location, membrane, metal, wood, effect, pitched, glass, other-percussion. Get the complete enumerations from the XSD, not from prose.
- MusicXML Standard Sounds - the full percussion instrument id list.
- MEI - Music Encoding Initiative - its percussion and playing-technique attributes.
- Dorico percussion playing techniques and its .doricolib playing-technique ids.
- Sibelius Sound Set editor guide and its SoundID vocabulary for unpitched percussion.
- Finale percussion maps and its percussion note-type vocabulary.
- Guitar Pro and MuseScore drum note-type lists.
- The Behind Bars notation reference by Elaine Gould - its percussion chapter.

## Note on this bucket

These are the sources that already had to enumerate playing techniques as a closed set. Extract the enumerations verbatim and completely.

Round A first. At least twelve genuinely different searches, varying register,
