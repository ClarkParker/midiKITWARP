# Bucket 05 — MMA and vendor normative MIDI documents

Read `.agents/round2/BRIEF.md` first. It defines the three-round method, the axis
model and the output structure. Follow it exactly.

## What this bucket must cover

- The MMA General MIDI 1 percussion key map and General MIDI 2 specification, including the RP-series recommended practices that amend them.
- The MMA MIDINameDocument DTD - .midnam - which Pro Tools, Logic, Digital Performer and Ardour all read. Get the DTD itself and the element list.
- Roland GS format specification and the SC-55 / SC-88 / SC-8850 drum set tables from Roland's own documentation.
- Yamaha XG specification and the XG drum voice lists from Yamaha's own data lists.
- DLS Level 1 and 2, and the MMA SP-MIDI and MIDI 2.0 documents, for anything that names percussion articulations.
- MPE and MIDI 2.0 per-note controllers, insofar as they bear on strike position and pedal position.
- The MMA's own manufacturer SysEx id list, as a precedent for identifier allocation.

## Note on this bucket

Round 1 got GM/GM2/GS/XG through secondary tables. Go to the normative documents and quote them. Where the normative document is paywalled, say so and use the most authoritative freely available restatement, marking it as such.
