# Bucket 08 — Marching and drum corps percussion

Read `.agents/round2/BRIEF.md` first. It defines the three-round method, the axis
model and the output structure. Follow it exactly.

## What this bucket must cover

- Percussive Arts Society marching percussion terminology and any published standard.
- Drum Corps International and WGI technique and instrumentation vocabulary.
- Marching snare technique names: gock, ping, rim shot, stick shot, backstick, cross stick, rim click, shell, buzz, and the exact meanings, which differ from kit usage.
- Tenor / quad terminology: the spock drums, shots, sweeps, crossovers, scrapes, and the numbering conventions for a tenor rack.
- Marching bass line: the unison hit, the numbering convention 1 to 5, and the split-versus-unison writing conventions.
- Marching cymbal technique names: crash, hi-hat crash, choke, tap-choke, bell tap, sizzle, zing, crunch, suc, orchestral crash versus hand crash.
- MuseScore's shipped marching drumset files as a cross-check on the names.

## Note on this bucket

Round 1 already found several of these names in MuseScore's marching drumsets without knowing where they came from. This bucket finds the authority behind them.
