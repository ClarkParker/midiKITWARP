# Bucket 03 — Organology and controlled instrument vocabularies

Read `.agents/round2/BRIEF.md` first. It defines the three-round method, the axis
model and the output structure. Follow it exactly.

## What this bucket must cover

- Hornbostel-Sachs classification, the full 1914 scheme and the MIMO 2011 revision, including the digit-by-digit meaning for membranophones (2) and idiophones (1).
- MIMO - Musical Instrument Museums Online - its instrument thesaurus and its keywords, which are a maintained controlled vocabulary with stable identifiers.
- Getty Art and Architecture Thesaurus musical instrument facet.
- Library of Congress Subject Headings and the LC Genre/Form terms for percussion.
- Wikidata percussion instrument items and their properties, and the Wikipedia list articles, as an index into named instruments.
- Grove Music Online / Oxford Music Online percussion entries.
- The Galpin Society and museum catalogue terminology.
- Any published instrument-name authority file with stable ids - this is directly relevant to KITWARP's own identifier policy.

## Note on this bucket

The question this bucket answers: is there an existing authority file KITWARP should cross-reference instead of inventing instrument names, the way SMuFL glyph names are already proposed as a cross-reference?
