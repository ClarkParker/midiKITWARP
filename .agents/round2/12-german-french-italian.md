# Bucket 12 — Non-English score and pedagogical terminology

Read `.agents/round2/BRIEF.md` first. It defines the three-round method, the axis
model and the output structure. Follow it exactly.

## What this bucket must cover

- German: Schlaginstrumente, Spielanweisungen, Schlagtechnik. Terms such as am Rand, auf der Kuppel, Rand schlagen, Fell, Zarge, Teppich ab, mit Besen, Schlegel, Klopfen, Wirbel, Vorschlag, Doppelschlag, gedampft, abgedampft.
- French: modes de jeu, sur le bord, au centre, sur la coupole, baguette, mailloche, balai, etouffe, roulement, sourdine, timbre.
- Italian: colpo, sul bordo, al centro, sulla cupola, bacchetta, mazzuolo, spazzola, rullo, sordina, con corde / senza corde.
- Spanish: tecnicas de percusion, golpe abierto, golpe sordo, palma, punta, borde.
- The standard multilingual glossaries that pair these, for example the Universal Edition and Barenreiter house style guides, IRCAM's instrument and playing-technique database, and the Vienna Symphonic Library instrumentology pages, which publish an extensive multilingual technique glossary.
- IRCAM Solomon / IRCAM Instrumental Technology database - it is an explicit, structured catalogue of playing techniques per instrument and is one of the most complete such catalogues in existence.

## Note on this bucket

Non-English score directions frequently name a distinction English collapses. The deliverable is a synonym concordance plus any technique that only the non-English tradition names.
